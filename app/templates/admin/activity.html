What counts as an interaction: a tap on a bot button, any command, a message in a managed group, a vote in a poll the bot itself created, or a direct message to a bot. One of them anywhere in the month is the whole requirement — extra ones never pay extra.

What cannot count: opening a group or reading a channel post. Telegram sends bots no event for either, so nothing here pretends to measure it.

Retention: the current month and the previous one — enough to run and audit a payout. Older months read as inactive because the data is genuinely gone; the withdrawals table is the permanent record of what was paid. See the audit log for status changes.
