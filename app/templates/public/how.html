How it works

Joining

Open the main bot through someone's referral link and tap the single button. That one tap shares your contact, verifies you, places you in exactly one group and one channel, and gives you your public ID and your own referral link.

Your phone number is stored only as a SHA-256 hash. It is never displayed, exported or shared — it exists so one person cannot hold two accounts.

Two levels of earnings

You earn on the people you invite and on the people they invite:

  • 🥇 Level 1 — ₹{{ '%g' % r.level1 }} for each active member you invited yourself.
  • 🥈 Level 2 — ₹{{ '%g' % r.level2 }} for each active member one of your referrals invited.

So if you invite B, and B invites C, D and E: you earn ₹{{ '%g' % r.level1 }} on B plus ₹{{ '%g' % r.level2 }} each on C, D and E — ₹{{ '%g' % (r.level1 + r.level2 * 3) }} in total — while B earns ₹{{ '%g' % r.level1 }} each on C, D and E.

It stops at two levels. Whoever C invites pays C at level 1 and B at level 2, but nothing to you.

What makes someone count

A member of your downline counts for a month when all three are true:

  • they are still a member of the channel,
  • they are still a member of the group,
  • they interacted with a bot at least once during that month — a button tap, a command, a message in the group, or a vote in a poll the bot posted.

Membership on its own never counts. Extra taps never pay extra: one interaction in the month is the whole requirement, and a thousand taps still pay once. If someone leaves either chat they stop counting immediately.

Everyone is judged on their own activity, so an inactive person at level 1 costs themselves their earnings but does not remove the people below them from your level 2.

To be paid at all you must meet the same bar yourself: in both chats, and at least one interaction that month.

Getting paid

Months run from the 1st at 00:00 IST to the last day at 23:59 IST. Send /bank to the main bot once to record your name, account number, IFSC and UPI id, then /withdraw to request that month's payout. An admin reviews requests and transfers by bank after the month closes.

What the bots cannot see

Telegram gives bots no way to know that someone opened a group or read a channel post — no such event exists in the Bot API. Nothing here claims to measure that. Activity is built only from events Telegram actually delivers: button taps, commands, group messages, poll votes on bot-created polls, and membership changes.
