# Privacy notice

This notice explains what the {{company}} app collects and why. The data controller is {{legal_name}}. Contact: {{support}}.

## What we collect

- **Mobile number and password.** To create and protect your account. Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash.
- **Telegram account ID and your Telegram phone number.** Only to check that the Telegram account belongs to the number you signed up with, and to keep one Telegram account per member. We don't store your Telegram contacts or messages.
- **Channel join requests.** Only to check the verification step.
- **Referral relationships.** Who referred you, and who you referred, to calculate rewards.
- **Bank details** (name, account number, IFSC). Only to pay your rewards. The account number is stored encrypted, and the app only shows its last 4 digits.
- **Technical data.** App version, and request logs kept for security and to fix problems. They never include your password, bank number or login tokens.

## What others can see

- People you referred directly can't see your details.
- You can see the people you referred directly, with their phone numbers masked (for example 98XXXXXX10). You can never see anyone further down.

## Advertising

The app may show ads from Google AdMob. Where required, you are asked for consent first. Ads are never shown during signup, verification or withdrawals.

## How long we keep data

Account and payout records are kept for as long as the law requires for financial records. Unverified signups are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

## Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting {{support}}. Records we must keep by law are retained until that period ends.
